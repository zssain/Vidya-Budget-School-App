What you'll need

  • UTR — the payment reference number your UPI app shows after a successful payment.
  • School name — exactly as you want it to appear in Vidya.
  • Computer code — the machine code Vidya shows on the "Set up my school" screen (looks like 7KQ2-M9XD-4TRA-P). It ties your licence to that computer.

Your licence is bound to one computer's code. If you later move to a new PC, ask us for a transfer licence.

The steps

  1. Pay by UPI

    Scan the QR above and pay [PRICE] from any UPI app.

  2. Note the UTR (payment reference)

    After the payment succeeds, your UPI app shows a UTR / transaction reference number. Copy it.

  3. Open Vidya and copy your computer code

    Install Vidya on the school's main PC (Downloads), open it and choose Set up my school. It shows Your computer code — tap Copy.

  4. Send the UTR + school name + computer code by email

    Use the button below — it opens a prefilled email to us. Just fill in your UTR, school name and computer code, then send.

    Send my payment details

    Or email us directly at mohammedzuhairhussain28@gmail.com with those three details.

  5. Receive your licence key

    We check the payment and reply with your licence key (and a .vlic file). Paste the key into Vidya's Licence key field, or load the .vlic file. Activation is instant and offline — setup continues with no internet.

Moving to a new computer? Recover on the new PC, note its computer code, and email us for a transfer licence. The old computer becomes read-only as soon as it next syncs.
