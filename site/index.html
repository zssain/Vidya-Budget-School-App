What Vidya is

School software that runs on your school's own computer.

Vidya is management software for small Indian schools (about 200–1,500 students, 5–60 staff). The Principal installs it on the school's main PC and that computer becomes the school's record. Staff join by invitation. Teachers take attendance and enter marks for their own classes; accountants handle admissions and fees; the Principal sees everything and approves corrections. English, Hindi and Telugu.

Attendance & marks

Teachers mark Present or Absent and enter marks for their own classes on a phone. Submitted sheets are locked; corrections go to the Principal for approval.

Fees & accounts

Accountants record payments, print receipts and see dues. Money is permanent — mistakes are fixed by an approved reversal, never a silent edit. Amounts in ₹ with lakh grouping.

Works offline

Every feature works with no internet. Phones and PCs reach the school over the school Wi-Fi, or exchange encrypted changes through the school's own Google Drive. No company server is ever involved.
