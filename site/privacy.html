Privacy

Privacy notice

How Vidya handles your school's data.

The school owns and controls its data

Vidya is software your school runs on your school's own computers and phones. Your school is the data fiduciary for the student, guardian, staff and financial information it holds. Vidya (the software) and Zuhair Hussain (the developer) do not operate a service that stores your school's data.

Vidya never sends your school's data to the developer

School data is stored on your school's own devices and, when you choose to sync, in your school's own Google accounts (a sync account and a private backup account that you control). It is encrypted before it leaves any device. Google, and the developer, cannot read it. No school data is sent to Zuhair Hussain or to any company server.

No analytics or tracking

Vidya contains no behavioural tracking, advertising or analytics SDKs. The app does not phone home. There is no online licence check and no remote switch-off.

What the developer does receive

When you buy a licence you send us, by email, your UPI payment reference (UTR), your school name and your computer's machine code. We use these only to issue and record your licence. We do not receive any student, guardian, staff or financial records.

This website

This website is a static site hosted on free static hosting (GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages). It loads only its own images and files from this domain. It does not set advertising cookies or run analytics.

Data rights inside the app

Vidya provides tools for the school to record guardian consent, export one student's data, and erase personal data on request where the law allows (financial and audit records are kept as required). These actions are performed by the school, on the school's own devices.
