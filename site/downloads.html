How to verify a download (SHA-256)

Some builds are unsigned. Before installing, check the file's SHA-256 checksum matches the value shown here.

  • Windows (PowerShell): Get-FileHash .\Vidya_<ver>_x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256
  • macOS / Linux: shasum -a 256 Vidya_<ver>_universal.dmg
  • Android: use a checksum app, or compute the hash on a PC after downloading.

Installing on Android

Android may warn that the app is from an unknown source. Open the downloaded APK, then when prompted tap Settings → Allow from this source (or Install unknown apps), turn it on for your browser or Files app, go back and tap Install. Choose the file that matches your phone — most phones use arm64-v8a; older 32-bit phones use armeabi-v7a.
